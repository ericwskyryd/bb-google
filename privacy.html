BB Personal Google Connector

Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Who uses it

This connector is a personal tool. Its only users are its owner and the owner's own Google accounts. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads Gmail messages, sends messages the owner asks it to send, lists calendars, and reads and updates calendar events. It also receives the account's email address to confirm which account is connected.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests, such as summarizing the owner's inbox or adding an event to the owner's calendar. When the owner works with an AI assistant, the relevant content is passed to that assistant as part of the requested task.

The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone other than the services the owner chooses to use for these tasks.

Where it is stored

The connector runs on the owner's own computer. Sign-in tokens are stored there in a private folder readable only by the owner's user account. The connector does not keep its own copy of mail or calendar content.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page. Revoking it stops all further access immediately.

Contact

Questions go to the owner through GitHub.
