BB Personal Google Connector

A private tool that lets its owner's own AI assistants read and send mail and manage calendar events in the owner's own Google accounts.

It runs only on the owner's computer, for the owner's personal accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, and has no sign-up.

Google access it requests:

  • Gmail: read messages, and send messages the owner asks it to send
  • Calendar: list calendars, and read and update events
  • Basic sign-in: the account's email address
